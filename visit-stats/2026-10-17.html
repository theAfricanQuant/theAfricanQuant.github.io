Method. Counts come from the self-hosted GoatCounter database at stats.sisengai.com, filtered to pageviews (events excluded), bucketed by hour and converted to Europe/Berlin days. The window runs 08:00 to 08:00 so it survives the daylight-saving switch. No cookies, no fingerprinting, no visitor identity.
